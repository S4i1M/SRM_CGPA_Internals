How the numbers work

Nothing is hidden behind a black box — the rules are the ones in SRM's academic regulations.

Pick your regulation

Theory courses under the 2021 regulation are assessed 60% internal + 40% end semester. Under the 2026 regulation it is 50% + 50%. Every tool switches with it.

Enter what you know

Internal marks, your paper total (75 by default), credits — whatever you have. Blank fields fall back to sensible defaults instead of breaking.

Read the answer

Marks needed, rounded up to a whole mark, with the percentage and the grade-by-grade table beside it. Everything stays in your browser.

Questions people ask

How accurate is the grade predictor?
It applies the published weightages and grade bands exactly, so the arithmetic is right. The one thing it cannot know is normalisation: SRM normalises internal marks before combining them with the end-semester marks, and that method is set by the Controller of Examinations. Treat the result as the marks you need under the standard formula — a very good estimate, not a promise.
What is the difference between the 2021 and 2026 regulations?
2021 regulation theory courses carry 60% internal weight and 40% end semester. From the 2026 regulation it is 50/50. The grade bands are identical in both: O 91–100, A+ 81–90, A 71–80, B+ 61–70, B 56–60, C 50–55, below 50 is a fail. Under the 2026 regulation you also need at least 45% in the end-semester exam itself to pass.
I don't know my CT2 marks yet — can I still use it?
Yes. On the internals page leave CT2 blank and tick “assume full marks” — that is what most people do between the two cycle tests. Untick it any time to enter the real score.
Does it store my marks anywhere?
No. Everything is calculated in the page and saved to your own browser's localStorage so the form is still filled in next time. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Clearing site data clears it.
Do my subject names or credits leave my device?
Never. The whole project is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript — you can open it from a downloaded folder with no internet at all and it behaves the same, apart from the web fonts.
