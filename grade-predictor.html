How this is worked out

Weightage. 2021 regulation theory courses are assessed 60% internal + 40% end semester; 2026 regulation courses are 50% internal + 50% end semester.

Paper scaling. Your paper is marked out of the total you enter (75 by default) and then scaled to its weight — 40 marks under 2021, 50 under 2026 — before it is added to your internals.

Grade bands (same in both regulations): O 91–100 · A+ 81–90 · A 71–80 · B+ 61–70 · B 56–60 · C 50–55 · F below 50.

Passing. A minimum of 50% of the total is required. Under the 2026 regulation you also need at least 45% in the end-semester exam itself — that is the stricter of the two numbers above.

The one caveat. SRM normalises internal marks before combining them with the end-semester marks, and the exact method is set by the Controller of Examinations. Treat this as a solid estimate of the marks you need, not a guarantee.
