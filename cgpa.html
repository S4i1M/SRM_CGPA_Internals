Useful to know

SGPA is weighted by credits: a 4-credit course moves your CGPA twice as much as a 2-credit one. That is why a single low grade in a big-credit subject hurts more than two small ones. A grade of F carries 0 points and stays in the credit total until you clear the course.

Once you know your internals, the grade predictor tells you the marks needed in the end semester for each grade — handy for picking the grades you enter here.
