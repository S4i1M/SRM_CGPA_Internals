How the scaling works

Each component is scaled to the marks it is worth: a score of 38 out of 50 that counts for 15 becomes (38 ÷ 50) × 15 = 11.4. Add the scaled values together and that is your internal total for the subject.

Under the 2021 regulation theory courses carry 60 internal marks; under the 2026 regulation they carry 50. Take the total from here to the grade predictor to see what it means for your grade.
